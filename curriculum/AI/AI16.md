## Overview

**AI16 is the cumulative graduation project for the entire required AI Engineering program.** The learner independently chooses an authorized, meaningful AI task and develops **one substantive, bounded and reproducible AI system** from a justified problem and model choice through source-safe implementation, independent comparative evaluation, accountable risk decision, actual CPU-local operation, controlled failure/recovery and an unfamiliar technical defense. **The one project begins with the charter in Module 1 and is refined through the entire course; Modules 2–5 do not begin unrelated mini-projects.** A completed notebook, model/API wrapper, confident generated explanation, cloud deployment badge or high benchmark score alone does not establish graduation-level ability.

**Capstones measure integration and independent transfer, not a fresh retest of every specialist exercise.** Before entry, the learner must already hold *authentic, reviewed assessed completion* of each required **AI1–AI15** course, or a governed **outcome-equivalent assessment decision** identifying the exact original revision, outcomes and reviewer. This is **a separate admission decision** from successful AI16 project assessment. The capstone cannot fabricate, repair by assertion, or replace missing prerequisite evidence. An optional AI17–AI20 elective, APP/SYS/SEC program, additional language, GPU, paid foundation model or external vendor is not a graduation condition.

**The explicit prerequisite graph matters.** The two *direct* dependencies are completed [AI6](AI6.md) and [AI15](AI15.md). AI15 inherits AI14 risk governance, AI13 cross-system evaluation, AI10 model training, AI11 retrieval and AI12 tool agents, their AI3/AI9 foundations and all applicable preceding AI1–AI5/AI7–AI9 knowledge. **It does not transitively require AI6**, which branches from AI3 and AI5. Therefore AI6 must be explicitly required rather than silently treating agent-tool work as reinforcement-learning mastery. The admission ledger independently checks **every** AI1–AI15 core prerequisite, irrespective of path inference. The curriculum may be studied in a valid prerequisite order rather than simply in numerical order; missing assessed decisions defer admission.

**Academic research and engineering context.** A culminating project should make earlier learning productive in an authentic design under multiple real constraints, with objective verification and a defensible individual contribution. ACM/IEEE-CS/AAAI CS2023 and ABET's published computing/engineering educational criteria discuss project synthesis, design, evaluation, professional judgment and communication. These are educational research references **not an assertion of ABET accreditation, credit equivalence or qualification**. NIST's AI RMF provides a useful non-normative comparison for combining use-specific evidence, stakeholder/rights review and accountable decisions. AI15 supplies local release/operational evidence rather than promising internet-scale production reliability.

**Project selection with real choice.** Suitable project forms include an **open-data classifier or forecasting assistant with a CPU-local serving interface**, a **source-grounded question-answering system with authoritatively checked citations and abstention**, a **bounded vision/language model with source-safe evaluation**, or an **observation-conditioned tool helper that changes only a simulated authorized resource**. These are alternatives, *not* a mandated menu, and one well-chosen narrower system is better than an untestable collection of AI features. A locally runnable model must genuinely compute a task-dependent result using authored, trained, adapted or permitted frozen weights, and the learner must contribute substantive independently reviewable engineering decisions. Reusing lawfully created earlier artifacts is allowed when the capstone introduces genuine new architecture, comparison, test, service and review work; a static answer template or opaque paid API endpoint alone is insufficient. A model may be a deliberately small classical, neural, transformer or other justified architecture; **RAG, agent tools, reinforcement learning, computer vision and all other specialties are not simultaneously mandatory implementation features**. Those prior course capabilities are established through their independently verified evidence and relevant transfer explanations.

**Safe and accessible environment.** Required work operates on an ordinary CPU using synthetic or legally usable small records, a genuine locally callable inference service and one open source/evidence collection. It may be implemented in a language/stack the learner can independently explain and test; no vendor, proprietary dataset, live protected person's data, GPU, cloud account, real irreversible external action or paid human reviewer is assumed. An independent educational reviewer is needed to **judge evidence and the unfamiliar change**; that reviewer can work from public source and accessible written/live discussion and need not be a paid mentor or credentialed examiner. Instructor-provided solution code, merely replayed transcripts or generated answers supplied in place of the learner's challenge are not qualifying performances. All numerical, model, interface, trace and risk evidence must remain independently inspectable and accessible without a visual-only dashboard. Ethical do-not-use or hold decisions are valid when a release would exceed permitted rights or evidence.

**Historical course identity protection.** On the still-published legacy `main` catalog, **AI9 meant “AI Engineering Capstone.”** On this **provisional expanded integration branch**, **AI9 now means “Transformers & Generative Modeling” and AI16 means “AI Engineering Capstone.”** These are **different course revisions, predecessor graphs, assessment standards and evidence identities**. Historic AI9 completion or project evidence **must not automatically convert** to new AI9 transformer completion, new AI16 completion or AI1–AI15 predecessor credit. Any historical transition requires separate outcome-by-outcome review, approved equivalence when justified, original evidence retention and downstream learner identity/curriculum consumer cutover. No legacy records are changed by authoring this draft, and the staging branch is not the published graduation catalog until the final atomic program release.

**New capstone competency and duration.** AI16 uniquely assesses whether the learner can *select, integrate, change, operate and independently defend a coherent AI system under realistic conflicting constraints*. AI1–AI15 own the substantive first assessment of their individual science/model/IR/RL/generative/agent/evaluation/governance/operational specialties; those are not copied into a feature checklist or a second 15-course examination. The five modules below follow the single-project lifecycle, with lesson boundaries determined only by cognitive work and observable evidence; **no module, lesson, project count or lab/test template** was selected in advance. Five cumulative assessment contracts measure the 16 capstone-specific outcomes plus authentic prior-program evidence. Duration **12–18 weeks** (roughly 120–270 focused hours at a provisional 10–15 hours/week) is an estimate covering real development, independent review, debug/repair and defense; it is not learner-calibrated or a time-based completion shortcut. The course is `draft`.

### Independent public finite oracles and limits

These finite reference cases are separate from the learner's **genuinely executed** project and may be replaced with equivalently reproducible justified independent oracles. They do not certify model quality, source rights, statistically significant improvements, large-scale reliability or actual end-user safety. Source, population, positive class, assessment revision and input permissions must be stated whenever applying them.

**S1 — Grouping before splitting.** Three synthetic primary sources **A, B, C** are each independent *source units*. Source A produces derived examples `A:1` and `A:2`; B produces `B:1`; C produces `C:1`. If a supposed independent held-out evaluation places `A:1` in training and `A:2` in test, the source **A** appears in both, so the **source-group separation invariant fails** regardless of whether the text hashes differ. The correct split must keep all derived examples from any source together. Independent source *groups*, not the four derived examples, are potential sampling units for uncertainty. This exercise checks source lineage and leakage discipline; it does not establish representative coverage for a large population.

**E1 — Genuine paired prediction comparison with uncertainty.** For six independent toy binary examples, exact reference labels are `[1,1,0,0,1,0]`. The declared deterministic baseline predicts `[1,0,0,0,0,0]`; the candidate model predicts `[1,1,0,1,1,0]`. Baseline has **four correct of six**, candidate has **five correct of six**. Paired correctness difference candidate−baseline is **[0,1,0,−1,1,0]**, yielding **two improvements, one regression, three ties** and absolute observed accuracy gain **1/6**. Under a two-sided **exact sign test** conditional on the three discordant pairs and equiprobable win/loss null, **p=1** (no reliable broad improvement established by this tiny fixture). A valid model comparison also requires source grouping, matched conditions, actual executed candidate output, original labels, uncertainty reporting and correction of any selection/tuning leakage. The toy numbers are not a target accuracy to optimize.

**R1 — Operating reliability does not follow from the model score.** A local service has a predeclared illustrative eligible-request success SLO of **99%** over exactly **200** eligible client requests. Allowed failed requests are **(1−.99)×200=2**. If three fail, the observed request success is **197/200=98.5%**, and error-budget consumption is **3/2=150%**. That service fails this stipulated operational gate even if its offline model accuracy improved on E1. This is *request-count* error-budget arithmetic, not a time-based burn-rate policy without a window, and an unauthorized write, a revoked source or a failing risk requirement separately vetoes promotion regardless of the raw availability score.

**V1 — An identified model/evidence release is not a mutable alias.** Suppose approved service source identifies **model digest `m1`**, feature transform **f1**, tokenizer **t1** if relevant, prompt/index version **r1** if relevant and access policy **a1**. A candidate silently changes feature transform to **f2**, source index to a revoked version or model digest to **m2** while still presenting `latest`. It is **not** the approved release and requires compatible evaluation, rights review and a new authorized promotion decision. A rollback that restores new-request routing to m1 must inspect real ready model/source state and cannot undo already executed external tool effects. These are schematic IDs, not implemented cryptographic hashes.

**U1 — Unfamiliar challenge must be predicted before execution.** Suppose the reviewer revokes one source that formerly supported a RAG answer, switches an agent tool's permission to read-only, alters a held-out grouping label, or injects an incompatible feature revision. The learner must first state which claim, computation, state transition, test denominator or release permission will cease to be valid; then change or refuse the code/action, observe an actual outcome and check unaffected cases. Merely saying that the model will “handle it safely” or generating a post-hoc explanation is not a prediction. The reference cases only specify a reasoning protocol; the reviewer chooses the actual bounded change after reviewing the candidate.

**Independent and accountable source claims.** An AI system may return a correct-looking response learned from parameters without evidential support; an agent may narrate a committed action without creating a real effect; a service may be live without being ready; an attractive overall accuracy may hide unacceptable subgroup outcomes. The learner must choose fitting independent oracles and a proportionate no-use/hold action where evidence or authority is missing. None of these cases requires an unsafe live target, private data or a proprietary foundation-model trial.
