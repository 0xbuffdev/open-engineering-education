# Mentorship

The curriculum in this repository is free and will stay free. Mentorship helps with the parts that are hard to do alone. We figure out what to study next, go through your code and designs, and work on the ideas that tutorials skip.

I take 3–5 one-to-one mentees at a time. Group cohorts run separately. If the spots are full, ask to join the waitlist.

## Who this is for

- self-taught developers who can build small things but freeze on a real project;
- career switchers who need an order to follow;
- students and junior engineers who want their code and decisions reviewed;
- engineers getting ready for a portfolio review, a promotion case, or interviews;
- people who can commit at least 8 focused hours a week and share their work.

## Who this is not for

- anyone who wants someone else to write their assignment or project;
- anyone looking for a job guarantee;
- anyone who will not share code, questions, or failed attempts;
- anyone who wants offensive security work outside an authorized lab or written scope.

## What a session looks like

Before the first session, I ask you to fill in a short intake. It covers where you are, what you have built, what you want in six months, and what is in the way.

Each session should leave you with one concrete result. We might:

- work out why a project is stuck and cut it down to the next few actions;
- review a pull request line by line;
- design a component or debug a failure together;
- rehearse an interview answer or review a portfolio;
- decide what to skip, what to study more deeply, and what to build next.

After each session I send written notes with the decisions, the action items, and the next check.
