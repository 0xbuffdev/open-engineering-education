# Mentorship

The curriculum in this repository is free and will stay free. Mentorship is the optional human layer: a realistic plan, feedback on your actual work, and help with the parts where tutorials stop.

I take **3–5 active mentees at a time**. If the spots are full, you can ask to join the waitlist.

## Who this is for

- self-taught developers who can build small things but freeze on a real project;
- career switchers who need a sequence, not another playlist;
- students and junior engineers who want their code and decisions reviewed;
- engineers preparing a portfolio, a promotion case, or interviews;
- people who can commit at least **8 focused hours a week** and share their work.

## Who this is not for

- anyone who wants someone else to write their assignment or project;
- anyone looking for a job guarantee;
- anyone who will not share code, questions, or failed attempts;
- anyone who wants offensive security work outside an authorized lab or written scope.

## How the offer is organized

Use the smallest plan that solves the current problem. Do not buy a multi-year package before you have tested the fit.

| Your situation | Start with | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You are unsure what to study next | **Roadmap Audit** | A one-off diagnosis is cheaper than months of drift. |
| One course lab or project is stuck | **Async Review** | A written review plus 30 minutes is enough to unblock most concrete problems. |
| You want steady accountability | **Guided Month** | Two sessions a month, with async review between them. |
| You are finishing one phase of a track | **Guided Sprint** | Twelve weeks gives enough time to build and review a real increment. |
| You want peers, not 1:1 | **Group Cohort** | Cheaper per person, with shared critique and deadlines. |

A full track is **not** a single mentorship SKU. Foundations is 720 hours; a specialisation after it is another 675–1,110 core hours. At 15 hours a week, that is roughly two to three years of consistent work. Buy one sprint, reassess, and continue only if the work is paying off.

There is no per-course mentorship rate. If you want help with one course, the **Async Review** ($100) or **Roadmap Audit** ($99) is the smallest useful unit.

## What a session looks like

Before the first session, you complete a short intake: where you are, what you have built, what you want in six months, and what is blocking you.

Each session has one concrete outcome. We might:

- diagnose why a project is stuck and reduce it to the next three actions;
- review a pull request line by line;
- design a component or debug a failure together;
- rehearse an interview answer or review a portfolio;
- decide what to skip, what to deepen, and what to build next.

After each session you get written notes: decisions, action items, and the next check.

## FAQ

**Is the curriculum included?**
Yes. The curriculum in this repository is free. 0xNAND, the interactive teaching platform, is still in development; mentorship does not promise a seat there. If that changes, it will be stated in the plan.

**Can I pay per course?**
No. The course content is separate. If you want help with one course, the Roadmap Audit or a Guided Month is the right entry point.

**Can I pay in NGN?**
Yes. Paystack supports NGN and international cards.

**Do you mentor security topics?**
Yes, within authorized labs and written scopes. We do not attack third-party systems.

**Do you guarantee a job?**
No. I can help you build evidence, prepare for interviews, and make better decisions. Hiring is not mine to promise.

**How long should I stay?**
Most people get value from a 12-week sprint or three months of monthly mentorship. You can stop at the end of any paid period.
