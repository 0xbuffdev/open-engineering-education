## Not covered by the local library

- F2 has no local DS&A book.
- F4 and AI1–AI12 have no local mathematics, data, ML, or AI book.
- D5 and D6 have no local cryptography or AI-security book.
- D1–D4 use some local systems material, but still require external threat-modeling and authorized hands-on labs.
- S11–S13 require modern distributed-systems, storage-engine, and AI-infrastructure material beyond the local PDFs.

Those gaps are intentional and are listed with legal acquisition routes in [resources/README.md](../resources/README.md). Do not commit downloaded or purchased books to the repository.
