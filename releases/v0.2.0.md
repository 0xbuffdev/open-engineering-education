# Open Engineering Education v0.2.0

**Release date:** 2026-11-07

**Status:** Draft specification and draft curriculum. This is not a claim of
learner-tested, verified, or production-complete course readiness.

OEE v0.2.0 introduces an **outcome-driven curriculum and assessment contract**.
Courses describe measurable capabilities and the evaluation evidence required
to demonstrate them, without prescribing an identical test/lab sequence for
every module.

## What's in this release

### Specification and governance

- Accept [RFC 0001](../rfcs/0001-outcome-driven-curriculum.md), establishing
  outcome-driven curriculum, proportionate assessment, and public evidence
  criteria.
- Revise the normative [Course Format](../spec/course-format.md),
  [Evidence Rules](../spec/evidence-rules.md), and
  [Conformance](../spec/conformance.md) to require stable identifiers
  for required course outcomes and summative assessments, with traceable
  outcome-to-assessment coverage.
- Remove universal per-module test/lab pairs and mandatory final-lab
  placement. Integration tasks remain appropriate where actual course
  outcomes require them.
- Preserve meaningful engineering reproducibility, appropriate project
  technical defenses, platform-independent public requirements, and the
  existing `draft`, `tested`, and `verified` readiness thresholds.

### Curriculum architecture

- Publish a [Curriculum Design Guide](../curriculum/DESIGN-GUIDE.md) defining
  focused course boundaries, cohesive modules, one primary learning
  objective per lesson, concise descriptive titles, and competency
  coverage across a program. Its suggested lengths and lesson counts are
  **editorial guidance**, not binding conformance constraints.

### Tooling and incremental migration

- Introduce the OEE v0.2.0 structural outcome validator and associated
  regression tests and GitHub Actions checks.
- Support legacy and v0.2.0 course definitions side by side in the SEF and
  APP program validators, retaining catalog, prerequisite, metadata, and
  link checks. A passing structural check is **not** sufficient to establish
  educational conformance or learner mastery.
- Pilot the redesigned course model with:
  - **SEF1 — Demystifying the Computer System:** eight cohesive modules,
    seven required course outcomes, and three evidence-based assessments.
    Essential digital-logic, storage, ALU, instruction, and I/O investigations
    remain.
  - **SEF10 — Software Engineering Foundations Capstone:** one cumulative
    developer utility across six project stages, eight outcomes, and three
    summative assessments covering design, engineering delivery, and
    independent technical defense.
  - **APP1 — Web & Internet Foundations:** fifteen modules, fourteen
    outcomes, and four assessments addressing network and DNS investigation,
    HTTP/TLS delivery, browser rendering/security, and end-to-end diagnosis.

These course definitions remain `draft` and require independent technical
review, learner evidence, and status-appropriate verification before any
readiness promotion.

## Compatibility and migration

**Breaking draft-spec change from v0.1.0:** New or explicitly migrated course
definitions that claim v0.2.0 conformance must use the revised outcome,
required-assessment, and evidence contract. Earlier published course
revisions remain associated with their original specification and
completion requirements; they are **not** automatically conforming to v0.2.0.

Unmigrated courses may retain the legacy test/lab structure until reviewed
and migrated. Consumer integrations, including OEE Workspace, resource
mappings, and 0xNAND, should update deliberately against versioned course
requirements. This release **does not** claim that all courses, validators,
workspace integrations, or instructional materials have been migrated.

## Scope and limitations

- OEE is an open educational specification and reference curriculum,
  not a certification authority or a paid learning platform.
- Published learning outcomes and evaluation criteria are public and
  do not require a commercial account.
- No learner completion, independently reproduced course, or
  `tested`/`verified` status is asserted by publication alone.
- Further APP program restructuring and broader curriculum migration
  remain separate ongoing work.
