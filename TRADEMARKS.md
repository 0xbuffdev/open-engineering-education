# Trademark Policy

This policy governs the use of names and marks associated with Open Engineering Education (OEE) and the products and services maintained by its steward.

It is intended to protect accurate attribution and prevent users from confusing compatibility with endorsement. It does not replace legal advice or the official registration records for the marks.

The content and code licences in this repository do not grant trademark rights.

## Registered marks

The following names are registered marks, as represented by the project steward:

- **Open Engineering Education®** and **OEE®**: the name and short form of the open engineering-education standard.
- **0xBUFF®**: the steward's house mark.
- **0xNAND®**: the paid reference implementation operated under the 0xBUFF brand.

0xNAND is part of the 0xBUFF brand family. Where the relationship is relevant, use wording such as "0xNAND, a 0xBUFF product."

Use the registered-mark symbol on the first prominent occurrence of a registered name in a document or announcement where practical. Later occurrences in the same document may omit it.

## Reserved conformance wording

The phrase **"conforms to OEE"**, and any logo or mark introduced to identify conformance, are reserved for future use.

Formal conformance criteria have not yet been finalised. Until they are defined and a review process is established, no person or organisation should claim to be officially certified as OEE-conformant.

Do not use the ® symbol for a future conformance mark unless that specific mark has been registered.

## Permitted use without special permission

Subject to the applicable content licences, the following are permitted:

- factual references to OEE;
- links to the OEE repository and its documents;
- descriptions such as "based on OEE" or "uses the OEE course format";
- accurate statements about compatibility or adaptation;
- quotations or adaptations of CC BY 4.0 material with the required attribution and licence notices;
- identification of 0xNAND as the paid reference implementation of OEE, where factually accurate.

Permitted references must not imply endorsement, certification, or official status that has not been granted.

## Use that requires permission or is not permitted

Do not:

- imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or official status without authorisation;
- use a registered name as the name of another product, course, company, or service in a way that creates confusion;
- use a reserved conformance mark before its criteria and authorisation process exist;
- falsely present a modified curriculum as the canonical OEE reference;
- remove or alter copyright, attribution, or licence notices;
- register or operate a domain intended to imitate or impersonate 0xBUFF or its products.

Nothing in this policy restricts rights expressly granted under the relevant copyright licences to copy, adapt, translate, teach, or redistribute the licensed content.

## Compatible implementations

OEE is intended to be usable by independent educators and organisations.

The trademark policy must not be used solely to block a compatible implementation that accurately describes its relationship to OEE and does not imply endorsement. The right to use the underlying content is governed by the content licence; the right to use the marks is governed separately by trademark law and this policy.

A compatible implementation may describe its material as "based on OEE" or "uses OEE principles" where that statement is accurate.

## Permission requests

To request permission for a use of a registered mark or reserved conformance mark, contact the steward through the official 0xBUFF contact channel.

A request should identify:

- the mark to be used;
- the product, course, or publication;
- the intended wording and visual presentation;
- the audience and distribution context;
- how compatibility or endorsement will be described.

Any permission granted should be recorded in writing.
